# PRISMA coverage check, by hand

**Why it is optional.** PRISMA has 30 m pixels, the same as EnMAP, so a 14 m dump fills the same small share of a pixel. It cannot change the plastic-content result. This check only closes the open item in the methodology. Stop if registration takes more than a few minutes.

## Steps

1. Register at https://prisma.asi.it and sign in.
2. Open the catalogue search and choose the L2 product. If several L2 products are offered, take L2D.
